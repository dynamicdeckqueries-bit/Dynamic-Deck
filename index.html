Good to know

Will it slow my machine down? +

No. DeckOS idles at under 1% CPU and about 60 MB of memory. Animations run on the GPU compositor, and the island pauses rendering whenever a full-screen app takes over.

What data does it collect? +

None leaves your device. Vitals, clipboard history, and media state are processed locally, there are no accounts, and clipboard entries are stored encrypted and can be wiped with one click.

Is it free? +

The core island, dock, and vitals are free. Custom themes and unlimited clipboard history are part of an optional Pro upgrade.
